1Purpose

This Statement of Work (“SoW”) is entered into between International Institute of Information Technology, Hyderabad (“IIIT-H”) and DHAROHAR CHARITABLE FOUNDATION (“Dharohar”) under the above-referenced MoU.

The purpose of this sponsored research project is to research, develop, evaluate and, where successful, deploy methods for automatically grouping pages from mixed manuscript collections into coherent candidate manuscripts for subsequent expert verification.

The project may additionally investigate ordering pages within a reconstructed manuscript where sufficiently reliable visual, structural or textual evidence is available. Within-manuscript page ordering is a stretch research objective and is not a core completion requirement.

2Nature of the Project

This is a sponsored research and technology-development project, not procurement of a pre-existing software product with a predetermined performance specification.

The research may investigate and combine physical and visual appearance, script and handwriting characteristics, page dimensions, layout and structural characteristics, line-level and document-image features, textual or content cues where sufficiently reliable and permitted, and relationships among pages within a mixed collection.

The project may use relevant existing IIIT-H technologies and Background IP where useful. Research directions and technical approaches may evolve based on experimental findings, collection characteristics and feedback from Dharohar experts.

3Scope of Work

  1. Jointly formulate the mixed-manuscript page-sorting problem and evaluation methodology with Dharohar.
  2. Establish appropriate research baselines.
  3. Research methods for extracting and combining relevant visual, structural and, where feasible, textual evidence.
  4. Develop and evaluate methods for grouping mixed manuscript pages into candidate manuscripts.
  5. Develop an expert-review mechanism supporting confirmation and correction of candidate groupings, including merge and split operations where appropriate.
  6. Investigate learning from expert feedback to improve subsequent model behaviour where technically appropriate.
  7. Investigate within-manuscript page ordering as a stretch research objective.
  8. Engineer a successful research prototype into a deployable implementation where the research produces a viable approach.
  9. Provide technical documentation, knowledge transfer and post-deployment support.

4Dharohar Participation and Data Access

Dharohar will provide representative mixed manuscript collections and available metadata; enable permitted access to manuscript images and related information; facilitate domain-expert review at mutually agreed stages; involve relevant engineers in technical discussions, evaluation and deployment; and provide infrastructure/access required for deployment where applicable.

Access and use of manuscript material will remain subject to participating-library, custodian and other applicable third-party restrictions communicated by Dharohar.

6Desired Research Target and Evaluation

The Parties will establish a mutually agreed evaluation set containing pages for which the correct manuscript groupings are known and which is reasonably representative of the collections targeted by the project. Where feasible, the evaluation set will be held separate from data used for model development.

Desired research target: B³ F1 of at least on the mutually agreed held-out evaluation set.

What B³ F1 means in plain language. For each page, the measure asks: (1) of the pages that the system placed in the same candidate manuscript, how many really belong with it; and (2) of all pages that truly belong to that manuscript, how many did the system successfully keep together. These correspond to precision and recall. Their balance is summarized by the F1 score.

Consequently, the measure penalizes both incorrect merging—putting pages from different manuscripts together—and incorrect splitting—separating pages that belong to the same manuscript.

The Parties may additionally examine expert correction effort, merge/split error patterns, and performance across different scripts, manuscript types, physical conditions and collection characteristics.

The selected target is a desired research outcome used to guide development and assess practical success; it is not a guaranteed performance level or warranty. As this is original research, completion is based on execution of the agreed research programme, delivery of the core deliverables and documented evaluation. Failure to achieve the desired target by itself will not constitute failure to complete the sponsored research project.

Metric reference: Amit Bagga and Breck Baldwin, “Entity-Based Cross-Document Coreferencing Using the Vector Space Model,” ACL/COLING 1998, pp. 79–85. DOI 10.3115/980845.980859. ACL Anthology.
